Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start your interior project.

Useful information for homeowners, offices, commercial spaces and industrial project enquiries.

Do you offer complete turnkey interior projects?

Yes. Turnkey scopes can combine design, materials, civil work, woodwork, ceilings, electrical, plumbing, furniture, finishing and coordination depending on the project.

Do you work in Jagatpura, Ramnagaria and Gyan Vihar?

Yes. These are key local service areas around the Jagatpura office, along with other Jaipur areas subject to project scope.

Can I hire you only for wallpaper installation or PVC wall panelling?

Yes. Individual services can be requested without a full interior package.

Do you design modular kitchens and wardrobes?

Yes. Kitchen layouts, cabinetry, storage, wardrobes and related furniture can be coordinated with the room design.

Do you provide 2D and 3D design?

Yes. 2D planning and 3D visualization can be part of the design scope depending on the project.

Do you undertake office and commercial interiors?

Yes. Office, shop, showroom, studio, clinic and other commercial interior requirements can be discussed.

Do you work on industrial projects?

Yes. Industrial building planning, factory design support, industrial interiors and facade-related requirements can be discussed.

Can you handle landscaping and garden work?

Yes. Landscape and outdoor-space planning is available for suitable residential and commercial projects.

How do I start?

Send your project location, property type, approximate area and the services you need using the enquiry form. The first discussion can then establish scope and next steps.

How are project prices decided?

Pricing depends on area, scope, materials, design level, site conditions and execution requirements. A project-specific proposal should be prepared after understanding the requirement.
