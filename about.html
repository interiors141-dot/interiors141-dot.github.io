About ORGANIC INDIA

Interior design with a build-and-execute mindset.

ORGANIC INDIA (OI BUILD & DESIGN) is positioned as a design, decoration and project-execution partner for residential, commercial and industrial environments in Jaipur.

Our approach

Beauty is only one part of a successful interior.

Our service mix is built around the full journey: understand the requirement, plan the space, visualize the design, coordinate materials and execution, and finish the environment with furniture, lighting, décor and details.

We work across individual service requirements as well as coordinated turnkey scopes. That makes the website and project process useful for a client looking for one room, a complete home, an office fit-out, an entrance makeover, an industrial building concept or an outdoor landscape.

Our process

A clear path from first conversation to handover.

The sequence can expand or contract depending on project scope.

01

Understand

Requirements, site, lifestyle, function, style, priorities and budget direction.

02

Plan

Measurements, layouts, circulation, storage and initial design direction.

03

Design

2D / 3D concepts, colours, materials, furniture, lighting and detailing.

04

Execute

Site coordination, vendors, materials, work stages, quality and finishing follow-up.

05

Handover

Final detailing, placement, snag review and ready-to-use project handover.

What clients get

A coordinated design experience.

Practical space planning

Layouts are developed around room function, movement, storage and daily use.

Visual clarity

2D and 3D concepts help align decisions around colour, materials, furniture and lighting.

Execution support

Project coordination connects design decisions with site work, vendors and finishing.
